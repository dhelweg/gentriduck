[I11] First post series — six grounded draft posts

## Why (problem)
A channel that starts and goes quiet is worse than no channel. The sustainable model for this
project is milestone-driven posting from findings that already carry sign-offs — and six such
stories exist today, unwritten.

## Goal
Six committed, signed-off, channel-ready draft posts (LinkedIn + Bluesky/Mastodon variants each),
ready for the maintainer to post manually on their own cadence.

## Scope & approach
Drafted by `comms-strategist` via the `comms-draft` skill, grounded exclusively in signed-off
sources:
1. **The re-check verdict** — a 2018 thesis, re-tested in 2026: what still holds, what changed
   (thesis-recheck narrative).
2. **The back-test** — the index finds all 8 literature-documented hotspots and all 6 stable
   controls (`docs/methodology/backtest.md`).
3. **The Offering-Advantage surprise** — *held until I15 passes* (fast-food signal story).
4. **The operating model** — a supervised team of AI agents with enforced review gates built this;
   what the gates caught (how-its-organised + `docs/process/`).
5. **The open-data story** — everything here runs on free public data; what that made possible and
   what was hard (I6 page).
6. **The timeline/launch post** — the project's arc 2018 → today (I4 page; pairs with I13's launch).
- Each: per-channel variants per the I9 format guidance; hook-first, simple but true; one honest
  caveat kept in-body; link to the site page/whitepaper; campaign-tagged URLs per I12.
- Drafts live in `docs/epic-i/posts/` (committed — the repo is public and transparency about
  outward messaging is consistent with the project's ethos).

## Acceptance criteria
- 6 drafts × 2 channel variants committed; every claim traceable to a signed-off source; register
  passes the I8 content rules.
- Per-post `*-comms-{domain,geo}-signoff.md` with Verdict: PASS (geo only where numbers are claimed).
- Post 3 blocked-marked until the I15 sign-offs exist.

## Gate / sign-off
Per-post dual sign-off (I8 gate). Maintainer decides if/when to actually post — posting is out of
scope for the agents.

## Dependencies / relations
After I8, I10; post 3 holds on I15; posts 5–6 reference I6/I4 pages (draft can precede the pages,
publish-readiness cannot).
