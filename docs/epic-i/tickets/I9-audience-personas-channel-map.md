[I9] Audience personas & channel map

## Why (problem)
"Optimize for reach within the target audience" is only meaningful once the target audiences are
written down: who they are, what they need from the project, where they actually are, and what
format lands with them. Today that exists only implicitly in the 2026-07-10 review's goals table.

## Goal
A committed audience & channel map: one persona per public goal-audience, with channel fit,
format guidance, and reach tactics — the reference every post draft (I11) is checked against.

## Scope & approach
- One persona each for: policy makers / city administration, neighbourhood initiatives, urban
  researchers, tech & AI practitioners, data engineers/analysts, open-data & civic-tech community.
  Persona = role, what they want to know, what convinces them, what alienates them, which pages
  serve them.
- **Personas are archetypes only — no real individuals, no organizations-as-targets, no contact
  data.** The map describes audience segments and public communities, nothing more.
- Channel fit per persona across the ADR-decided channels (LinkedIn, Bluesky/Mastodon) + owned
  surfaces (site, repo, whitepaper); format guidance per channel (length, tone, visual, hashtag/
  community conventions); posting-time/cadence heuristics grounded in public best-practice sources
  (WebSearch, cited).
- Reach tactics that respect the O3/O4 register: being genuinely useful and citable beats
  promotion; cross-linking the whitepaper/dataset for researchers; reproducibility as the hook for
  the data audience.
- Deliverable: `docs/epic-i/audience-channel-map.md`.

## Acceptance criteria
- Map committed with all six personas, channel fit, and per-channel format guidance; sources cited.
- Contains no personal data of any individual and names no outreach targets.
- I11's skill/checklist references it.

## Gate / sign-off
domain-expert reviews (audience framing, misuse angles). comms-strategist authors once I10 lands
(PM/data-analyst may draft earlier if sequencing demands).

## Dependencies / relations
After I8 (channels decided). Feeds I11 (drafts) and I12 (what "reach" means per audience).
