## Scope of review

This sign-off covers the theory-grounding and public-framing/ethics dimension of the whitepaper
content: whether the restated methodology stays faithful to Dangschat (1988) and Döring & Ulbricht
(2016), whether the scope-of-claims discipline (no unobserved displacement-event assertions,
ecological-inference guard) is preserved verbatim, and whether the Ethics section is an honest
statement of what the index can and cannot claim.

## Assessment

### 1. Theory grounding (Introduction, Methods)

- The double invasion-succession cycle (Dangschat 1988) is stated correctly: two coupled cycles
  (social, then commercial), with the thesis's confirmed finding (H3b, p. 91) that the social cycle
  *leads* — this is the load-bearing reason for the lead-lag architecture and the whitepaper states
  it as such, not as an incidental detail.
- Döring & Ulbricht's (2016) demographic-composition reading is correctly summarized as a
  *precondition* (susceptibility marker), not an ongoing outcome — this distinction is exactly the
  one that justifies the levels-vs-changes discipline on D4, and the whitepaper preserves it rather
  than collapsing "vulnerability at baseline" and "gentrification in progress" into one idea.
- The explicit statement that Smith's rent-gap (the economic/capital driver) is **absent** from
  D1-D4 is preserved and placed in both the Introduction and the Limitations section. This is the
  single most important scope-of-claims boundary in the whole document — the index measures the
  *social outcome* and its *correlates*, not the economic mechanism — and it is not soft-pedaled or
  buried.

### 2. Scope-of-claims / ethics discipline

- **G-1 (no displacement-event assertion) is preserved verbatim.** The whitepaper explicitly states
  that stage names use risk/pressure framing and that "post-displacement" is a prohibited name,
  correctly attributing this to the governed guardrail rather than presenting it as a stylistic
  choice.
- **G-2 (ecological-inference / small-area-aggregate disclaimer) is preserved and generalized
  correctly** across stages, hotspot labels, and cluster classifications — the Ethics section states
  it as a cross-cutting rule ("every stage, hotspot, and cluster label... never a confirmed
  outcome"), which is the right level of generality; it does not narrow the guard to only one
  model output.
- **Milieuschutz framing is correct and non-trivial to get right.** The whitepaper states the
  overlay is a *policy marker* that is simultaneously a Senate-identified risk signal *and* its own
  suppressive intervention — not an independent proxy measurement. This is exactly the nuance
  `index-definition.md` §1.8 requires and a naive restatement would easily have gotten wrong
  (e.g. by treating designation as if it were itself evidence of displacement).
- **The "improving trajectory" ambiguity caveat** (a numerically-improving D1 trajectory may
  reflect completed gentrification with displacement, genuine incumbent social mobility, or early
  pioneer-stage succession — mechanism is undetermined without D5) is present in the Limitations
  section, correctly citing that D5 (#70) is required for disambiguation. This is a caveat that is
  easy to omit in a summary document and its presence here is a positive finding.
- No claim in the document asserts the index measures displacement directly, asserts a causal
  mechanism the model cannot support, or presents a PLR-level statistic as an individual-level
  fact.

### 3. Data sources & licensing (ethics-adjacent)

Confirmed the document does not introduce or imply use of any paid, proprietary, or personally
identifiable data source; all listed sources are public administrative or crowd-sourced open data
under the project's approved licence families. This matches the project's open-data ethics
commitment.

### 4. Public-facing tone

The document is written for external/academic scrutiny (its stated goal) without overclaiming: it
states directional findings, cites conditions and caveats prominently rather than in fine print,
and repeatedly routes the reader back to the canonical, dual-signed-off methodology documents
rather than asserting new authority. This is the correct posture for a citable whitepaper that must
outlive any single contributor's framing choices.

## Conditions on integration (non-blocking)

None. The restatement is faithful and the scope-of-claims discipline (G-1/G-2, Milieuschutz
framing, rent-gap absence) is preserved without dilution.

## Verdict: PASS
