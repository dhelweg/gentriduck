## 1. Summary

I traced OA end-to-end from the thesis SQL (`70_oa_helper.sql` city-reference helper, `71_oa.sql`
per-PLR OA) and confirmed it is a **three-level nested location quotient** with a **parent-relative**
reference base: a domain is normalized against the all-domains total, a category or type against its
**parent domain** (every category/type column in `71_oa.sql` divides by `d_<domain>_stock`, not by
the grand total). This is the standard LQ construct and is well-posed on both POI variants.

The spike settles the two blocking questions:

1. **Method (how to compute OA on weighted stocks).** Compute the mass-conserving weighted stock
   **first**, aggregate up the taxonomy within each PLR and city-wide, and form the LQ **last**. The
   denominator must be the city-wide **weighted** totals per level within the same variant — never a
   hard-count denominator against a weighted numerator. I derived and record the key result: because
   the kernel weights `ŵ_ij` sum to 1 per POI and each POI keeps its taxonomy labels, the city-wide
   weighted total per level **equals** the hard-count city total per level. So the OA denominator is
   invariant across variants; the kernel only smooths the local compositional numerator. This is an
   enforceable dbt test (`Σ_a weighted_count_level = hard city total_level`) and is the correctness
   anchor for OA-A.2.

2. **Bandwidth.** 500 m is a pedestrian **density-gradient** radius, not a retail-service catchment.
   OA measures the competitive/complementary offering mix around a location, which retail-geography
   theory (Reilly 1931; Huff 1964; Berry 1967) places at the 1–2 km scale. The thesis OA itself used
   an effectively **unbounded** centroid-to-centroid distance spread, so a 500 m truncated Gaussian
   under-smooths OA relative to the golden. **Recommendation: OA sweep {500, 1000, 1500} m with a
   1000 m headline**, hard point-in-polygon as the bandwidth-free floor; report divergence as the
   ADR-0008 §4 mandatory sensitivity sweep.

Full method decisions and citations are in `spatial-methods.md` §11 (§11.1 method, §11.2 bandwidth,
§11.3 leakage guard, §11.4 discharge table).

The methodology is sound; this is PASS WITH CONDITIONS (not unconditional) because two of the
findings are **binding implementation/interpretation conditions** on the downstream build tickets,
not merely observations.

## 2. Methodological assessment

### 2.1 Order of operations — weight first, LQ last. Sound.

A location quotient is a ratio of shares. On smoothed stocks, the LQ must be formed **after** the
kernel: `ratio-of-smoothed ≠ smooth-of-ratio`. Forming per-POI shares before the kernel, or
smoothing an already-formed OA, would let a high-LQ neighbour bleed a spurious advantage across the
PLR border — precisely the edge artefact the mass-conserving design is meant to avoid honestly. The
weighted model already stops at `weighted_count`; OA-A.2 aggregates that up the taxonomy and divides.
Correct.

### 2.2 Denominator — city-wide weighted totals, same variant. Sound, with a proof-carrying test.

The denominator (the `70_oa_helper` city reference) must come from the same weighted universe as the
numerator or the LQ loses its unit-free "=1 is average" meaning. The mass-conservation invariance
(§11.1) means the weighted city totals equal the hard city totals per level — so the two variants
share a denominator and differ only in the local numerator. This is a genuinely useful result: it
makes the hard-vs-weighted OA comparison (plan decision 3) a clean numerator-only contrast, and it
gives OA-A.2 a hard equality test rather than a soft tolerance check.

### 2.3 Completeness-bias interaction — different from the D3/C5 rule. Correctly distinguished.

§5's "C5 first, weighting second" rule governs the D3 dynamism *spatial share* time-series and does
**not** transfer to OA mechanically, because OA's denominator is a **within-PLR compositional** share,
not a share-of-city-total. OA has its own, milder robustness: the LQ is **invariant to uniform
PLR-level coverage scaling** — proportional under-mapping of a whole PLR cancels in the ratio. The
real residual threat is **category-differential** completeness and tag-schema drift over time, which
bites the lagged `prev_oa_*` predictors (H2/H3) much more than cross-sectional `oa_*`. This is the
correct, non-alarmist framing and is why I do not require a C5-style pre-normalization for OA — but
it is a real temporal-comparability caveat for the lagged predictors (condition C-2).

### 2.4 Bandwidth — 500 m too narrow for OA. Well-grounded.

The density-layer 500 m default is defensible for D1/D3 (walkable amenity radius) but is the wrong
scale for a *retail-offering* catchment. The recommendation to widen to a 1000 m headline with a
{500, 1000, 1500} m sweep is grounded in (a) retail-gravitation/central-place theory, (b)
faithfulness to the thesis's unbounded distance spread, and (c) the mass-leakage mechanism below. It
is framed as a default-plus-mandatory-sweep, not a hard assumption, consistent with §4/§7 discipline.

### 2.5 Mass leakage in large PLRs — a real defect at 500 m; guard required. Blocking-for-A.2.

At `b = 500 m` the `ST_DWithin` truncation in `int_osm_poi_plr_weighted` drops any POI more than
500 m from **every** PLR representative point. In Berlin's large low-density PLRs (Tempelhofer Feld,
Grunewald, Flughafensee) this silently zeroes `total_weight` → NULL `normalized_weight` → the POI
vanishes, so mass is **not** conserved there and the OA denominator base inside those PLRs is
distorted. This affects the *current* weighted model, not just OA, but it is most damaging to OA
because OA forms its base within the PLR. OA-A.2 must add the §11.3 leakage guard (fall-back to the
POI's hard home PLR at weight 1, using the `hard_area_code` already carried in the model). Widening to
1000 m reduces but does not eliminate the need for the guard. This is condition C-1.

### 2.6 Scope discipline

Docs-only spike; no model or analysis code changed. The `weight_variant` discriminator pattern is
reused for the new OA bandwidths (`gaussian_1000m`, `gaussian_1500m`) so OA runs are never blended
with the density-layer variants or across the faithful/improved workstreams (plan §"Experimental
design"). Bandwidth stays a per-city `poi_kernel_bandwidth_m` var (ADR-0005). No new tool/source; no
ADR needed for the spike itself (the decisions feed the OA-P0.2 ADR #164).

## 3. Conditions

All conditions bind on the downstream OA build tickets before OA output is integrated or published.

- **C-1 [Required, blocks OA-A.2 #166].** Implement the §11.3 mass-leakage guard: any POI with a
  zero in-bandwidth weight sum falls back to its hard home PLR (`hard_area_code`) at weight 1. Ship
  the §11.1 invariance dbt test (`Σ_a weighted_count_level = hard city total_level` per year/level,
  to floating tolerance) as the guard's proof. Without the guard, OA in large low-density PLRs is
  biased and mass is not conserved.

- **C-2 [Required, labelling — OA-A.4 #168 / C.1 #174].** The lagged `prev_oa_*` predictors carry a
  category-differential OSM completeness / tag-schema-drift caveat that the cross-sectional `oa_*` do
  not. State this wherever H2/H3 OA lead-lag results are reported; do not read a weak/attenuated
  lagged-OA effect as a clean null (same attenuation-toward-null logic as the C5/B7 caveat).

- **C-3 [Required, OA-P0.2 ADR #164].** The ADR must record: (a) OA is a parent-relative nested LQ
  (domain vs all-domains; category/type vs parent domain), (b) weighted-stock-first / LQ-last order,
  (c) same-variant weighted denominator with the mass-conservation invariance, (d) OA headline
  bandwidth 1000 m with the {500, 1000, 1500} m sweep and the hard variant as floor. These are the
  spike's locked decisions; the ADR formalizes them.

- **C-4 [Advisory].** If the {500, 1000, 1500} m OA rankings prove bandwidth-fragile (e.g. low
  rank correlation across bandwidths), the G2 methodology page must flag OA as bandwidth-sensitive,
  mirroring the §7 r > 0.7 MAUP publish gate. Report the cross-bandwidth OA rank correlation in C.1.

- **C-5 [Gate].** This ticket is methodology-bearing (R-C1). Integration into `develop` also requires
  the independent `gentrification-domain-expert` PASS on the OA construct (is the parent-relative LQ
  and the 1000 m retail catchment theoretically appropriate for the Berlin gentrification reading?).

## 4. Risks

1. OA-A.2 ships without the leakage guard → biased OA and broken mass conservation in large PLRs
   (mitigated by C-1 + the invariance test).
2. A reader treats a weak lagged-OA (`prev_oa_*`) H2/H3 result as a null despite category-differential
   completeness drift (mitigated by C-2).
3. The faithful Run 1 OA backtest diverges from the 2018 golden partly because the thesis used an
   unbounded distance spread and the revival truncates at a finite bandwidth — a *methodological*
   divergence to document (Epic B directional framing), not a data discrepancy, and a further reason
   the OA headline uses the wider 1000 m rather than 500 m.
4. Blending OA `weight_variant` values across bandwidths or across faithful/improved workstreams
   would confound "wider catchment" with "world changed / metric changed" (mitigated by the
   variant-tagging + three-separated-result-sets discipline).
5. Domain-expert co-sign still owed (C-5); the LQ/catchment choices are theory-laden and need the
   urban-sociology gate before OA reaches `develop`.

## 5. Certification

The OA-on-weighted-stocks methodology is sound, faithful to the thesis LQ construct, and adequately
grounded (R-C2 citations for the LQ definition, retail-catchment scale, and the mass-conservation
invariance are in `spatial-methods.md` §11 and its Sources). The weight-first/LQ-last order,
same-variant weighted denominator, and the mass-conservation invariance are correct and give the
downstream build an enforceable correctness test. The 500 m bandwidth is correctly judged too narrow
for the OA catchment; the {500, 1000, 1500} m sweep with a 1000 m headline is well-grounded and
framed as a default-plus-mandatory-sweep. The one real defect surfaced — silent mass leakage in large
PLRs at finite bandwidth — is captured as a blocking implementation condition (C-1) with a concrete,
cheap guard. Conditions C-2 through C-5 are labelling/ADR/gate guardrails.

`gentrification-domain-expert` PASS required by the R-C1 dual gate). OA-A.2 (#166) must not be
integrated until condition C-1 is satisfied.
