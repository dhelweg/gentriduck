## 1. Findings

1. **"most of them no longer hold" overstates.** The triage shows 6 of 13 §4 verdicts flipping,
   which is fewer than half. The other 7 are not confirmed either: the surviving H2/H3c Spearman
   rows rest on analytic p over pooled, non-independent rows, which the findings already flag as
   weak. The accurate statement is the count plus that qualifier.
2. **The `/thesis-recheck` note must say that the page's own verdicts are unaffected.** That is
   true (triage §1: only the two "+ D4" sensitivity rows move, with no verdict change). Without the
   sentence, readers of a correction placed on that page will assume its results changed. Saying so
   explicitly is the honest framing, not a minimisation, because the reason is stated.
3. **The E1 banner must also cover the Overall Scorecard.** A banner at §4 alone leaves the
   Scorecard's "EWR resident-register composite: Supported 13/13" and "Cross-lagged (EWR): both
   directions supported" uncorrected. These are the lines a reader is most likely to quote.
   `methodology.md:468` and `methodology-oa-modes.md:750` link the same file for §1 (H1, rho +0.15),
   which is unaffected, so the file-top note must say which sections are fine.
4. **The download line needs a home and a scope.** No public "downloads" text exists. The serving
   parquet is the site's bundled data, and `/open-data` is the data-sources page, so the line goes
   there. It must say:
   - what is affected: Berlin shares and averages for 2008–2020;
   - what is not: resident counts, 2021+ values, and the figures the pages show;
   - and not imply that the counts are error-free in general, since #372 is a separate open issue.
     The sentence below is therefore scoped to "this error".
5. **Tone.** No alarm framing: no "warning", "invalid" or "retracted". "Correction" and "known
   error" are right. Keep "please do not cite … until re-run", which is the honest request.
6. **Must-not check.** The texts make no new substantive claim. They do not say the thesis is
   refuted or confirmed, do not name areas, and contain no displacement or pressure wording.
