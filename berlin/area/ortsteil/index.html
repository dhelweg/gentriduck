Chapter 3 — The Evidence

Ortsteile (Stadtteile)

Berlin's 97 Ortsteile — a non-LOR, legally-defined district subdivision distinct from the Planungsraum/Bezirksregion/Prognoseraum ladder used elsewhere on this site.

Each Ortsteil page shows population and composition sums (never a re-scored index — see the methodology page) and how many of its dominantly-assigned constituent neighbourhoods (Planungsräume) currently sit in each gentrification stage. Two Ortsteile — small enclaves that are never the largest-share (dominant) assignment for any Planungsraum — show 0 constituent PLRs below and render an explicit empty state on their own page rather than a misleading zero; see the district & area profiles hub for the Bezirk/Prognoseraum/Bezirksregion ladder, or the full neighbourhood list for individual Planungsräume.
